# Account Resource Days Queues Hours Minutes Per Queue (M) Combined / Iteration (M) Total Hours Iteration Hours Full Iterations Total Gathered (M) Remove row

“Hours” and “Minutes” are the time between collection / return / resend cycles. “Per Queue (M)” is your estimated haul for one march each cycle. Use the actual ETA and haul for your heroes, research, and mine level. Use “Other” when you have a third account or want to label a non-standard resource.

Combined / iteration = Queues × Per Queue (M)
Total hours = Days × 24
Full iterations = floor(Total hours ÷ Iteration hours)
Total gathered = Combined / iteration × Full iterations
Resource strategy: A farm account is meant to supply whichever resources your main account is short on—not necessarily the same resource forever. A Kingshot farm guide suggests starting with Bread and Wood, then shifting toward Stone and Iron as upgrade costs change; it also recommends keeping marches gathering consistently. At TC22, choose the resource based on your next upgrade or research bottleneck. For an 8-hour buff, use your own march ETA and per-queue haul rather than assuming the same schedule works for every mine level.
